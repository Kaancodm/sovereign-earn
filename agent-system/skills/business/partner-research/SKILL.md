# Purpose
Turn a broad partnership target into a qualified, evidence-based shortlist.

# Workflow
1. Define partnership objective and qualification criteria.
2. Research candidate organizations using approved read-only sources.
3. Assess relevance, likely value, fit, risks, and evidence quality.
4. Where appropriate, identify relevant professional contacts through approved enrichment tools.
5. Rank candidates and explain why each qualifies.
6. Produce outreach recommendations; do not contact candidates automatically.

# Output
- Candidate
- Why relevant
- Evidence
- Potential value
- Risks/unknowns
- Suggested contact role
- Recommended next action

# Safety
Research only. No email, message, CRM mutation, financial commitment, or external action without an approved downstream workflow.
